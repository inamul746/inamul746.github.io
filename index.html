About LuitBoard

LuitBoard is a mock test app built specifically for students preparing for Assam's competitive government and banking exams. With thousands of practice questions, full-length mock tests, and instant results, LuitBoard helps candidates prepare effectively for exams like ADRE, Apex Bank recruitment, DME, JAA and more.
