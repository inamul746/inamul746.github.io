When you sign in with Google, we collect:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create your account

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Firebase Firestore.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data.

6. Your Rights

You can request to delete your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send you push notifications about:

  • New mock tests available
  • Exam reminders
  • Test results
  • Important announcements

You can opt out of push notifications at any time by changing your device settings.

8. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our app. This helps us:

  • Improve app performance
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Understand which features are most used

No personally identifiable information is shared.
